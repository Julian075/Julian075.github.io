PhD student · Computer Vision Center, Barcelona

I am a PhD student at the Computer Vision Center (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona), supervised by Alexandra Gomez-Villa, where I work on color understanding in multimodal models. I also collaborate with Jesus Malo at Universidad de Valencia on generative models and vision science.

I hold an M.Sc. in Engineering and a B.Sc. in Electronics Engineering from Universidad de Antioquia, where my research focused on domain adaptation and foundation models for wildlife monitoring.
