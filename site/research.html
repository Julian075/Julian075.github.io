ECCV 2026

DriftScope: Measuring the Hidden Effects of Diffusion Model Adaptation

Fine-tuning or unlearning a text-to-image diffusion model also damages concepts it was never meant to touch, and standard metrics such as FID and KID do not show it. DriftScope takes two model checkpoints and returns a ranked list of the tokens whose visual concepts shifted the most, without real data or access to model internals.

M.Sc. research · Universidad de Antioquia

Wildlife monitoring and domain shift

Camera traps and acoustic sensors produce large amounts of data for biodiversity monitoring, but models trained in one place often fail in another. My master's work studied how foundation models and multimodal learning can close that gap. It included research stays at Télécom Paris (domain adaptation) and Universidad de los Andes (wildlife image segmentation).

IJCV 2026

WildIng: A Wildlife Image Invariant Representation Model for Geographical Domain Shift

Models trained on camera-trap images from one region lose accuracy when deployed in another. WildIng learns representations that stay stable across geographical domains.
